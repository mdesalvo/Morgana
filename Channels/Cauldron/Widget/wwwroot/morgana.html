From the potting bench

Three things we say most days

Nobody kills a plant on purpose. Almost everything that comes back to us on the counter comes back for one of these three reasons.

  • Too much water, not too little. Push a finger in to the second knuckle. Dry? Water it. Damp? Walk away.
  • Light is a quantity, not a mood. "Bright indirect" means it should read a newspaper's worth of light all day.
  • Repot in spring, never in the dark. A plant moved in November has to grow roots on an empty tank.
